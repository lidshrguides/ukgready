Canceling a Time Off Request

How to cancel a time-off request — before or after approval

Overview Steps
Overview

Two Scenarios

The process is different depending on whether your request has been approved yet.

📍 Corporate and DC employees only
Process

How to Cancel

Select your scenario below.

1

Go to Time Off History

Navigate to Menu ☰ > My Info > My Time > Time Off > History.

2

Select the Date(s)

Select the applicable date(s) for the request you want to cancel.

3

Click the Trash Button

Click the trash icon on the request. Once deleted, the request is immediately removed.

1

Go to Your Current Timesheet

Navigate to Menu ☰ > My Info > My Time > Timesheet > Current Timesheet.

2

Select Change Request

Click Change Request.

3

Select Cancel Time Off

In the Change Type field, select Cancel Time Off.

4

Choose the Request to Cancel

Select the time-off request you want to cancel.

5

Click Submit

Click Submit. Cancellation is submitted immediately and your manager will be notified.

⚠️
You will NOT receive a confirmation email. The cancellation is immediate once submitted.
