How to Initiate Background
Check & Preboarding

A step-by-step reference for moving candidates through background check and into preboarding

When to Use Initiate Background Check Move to Preboarding What Happens Next Critical Reminders
Overview

When to Use This Guide

Use this guide immediately after a candidate has accepted their offer.

✅

A candidate has accepted their offer and is ready for the next step

🔍

You need to initiate a background check through UKG Ready

📋

You are ready to move the candidate into preboarding

Step 1

Initiate Background Check

Not all positions require a background check. If you do not see the Initiate Background Check stage in the hiring pipeline, skip this section entirely and go straight to preboarding.

💡

Don't see the Initiate Background Check stage? Skip this section and go directly to the Move to Preboarding section below. Not all roles require a background check.

  1. 1
    Open the candidate's application Navigate to the requisition, click the Applications tab, select the candidate, and click View.
  2. 2
    Click the Initiate Background Check hiring stage circle This moves the candidate into the background check stage. Leave the date as-is and click OK to confirm the stage change.
  3. 3
    Scroll down to the Background Screen section You will see a section labeled "Background Screen" with the package listed as Basic Package + Adjudication.
  4. 4
    Click Initiate Background Screen A confirmation pop-up will appear: "You are about to send a background screen invitation to [candidate name]."
  5. 5
    Click Send This sends the background check invitation to the candidate. They will complete it on their end through HireRight.
Step 2

Move to Preboarding

Move the candidate to Preboarding after the background check has been initiated — or immediately if no background check is required.

  1. 1
    Open the candidate's application If you just completed the background check step, you are already in the right place.
  2. 2
    Click the Preboarding hiring stage circle Leave the date as-is when the pop-up appears.
  3. 3
    Click OK to confirm the stage change The candidate is now in Preboarding and will receive their preboarding checklist automatically.
What to Expect

What Happens Next

Once the candidate is moved to Preboarding, the process is automatic. Here is what happens on their end.

📬

Candidate receives a preboarding checklist

UKG Ready automatically sends them a checklist of tasks to complete before their start date.
↓
📝

Candidate completes the preboarding form

They are required to provide their Social Security Number (SSN) or Social Insurance Number (SIN) for Canadian employees, and their Date of Birth. This information is collected securely through UKG Ready.
↓
🔒

Information is submitted and stored securely in UKG Ready

This is required before the candidate can be officially hired. Do not attempt to collect this information outside of UKG Ready.
⚠️
This information is required before we can hire.

The candidate must complete the preboarding form — including SSN/SIN and Date of Birth — before they can be moved to the Hired stage. Do not move them forward until this is complete.

Critical Reminders

Critical Reminders

SSN and SIN are among the most sensitive categories of personal information. These rules are non-negotiable.

  • !

    SSN/SIN and Date of Birth are sensitive personal information — treat them with the highest level of care

  • !

    Do not request SSN, SIN, or Date of Birth outside of UKG Ready — never ask for it verbally, by text, or by email

  • !

    Do not store this information in email, personal files, spreadsheets, or any system outside of UKG Ready

  • !

    Access this information only for the purpose of the hiring process — no other use is permitted
