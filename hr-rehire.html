Overview

How Rehires Work at Lids

Rehiring a former employee follows the standard recruiting workflow — with a specific HR Actions step that happens after the background check clears.

The Rehire Process at a Glance

①Former employee applies, interviews, and receives an offer — exactly like a new hire.
②Background check is initiated and must clear before proceeding.
③Once the background check clears, go to Employees → Manage → HR Actions and click Start next to Rehire.
④Search for the employee's name, set Account Status to Active, enter the rehire date, then follow the Hire an Employee Guide for position, compensation, and all remaining steps.
Recruiting

Recruiting Steps

These steps are identical to the standard new hire process. No changes are needed for rehires at this stage.

1
Application

Former Employee Applies

  • The former employee applies through the career portal like any other candidate.
  • There are no shortcuts — they must submit a full application and go through the normal process.
2
Interview

Take Them Through the Interview Process

  • Review the application and move the candidate through your standard recruiting workflow stages.
  • Conduct interviews and advance stages as you normally would for any candidate.
3
Offer

Send the Offer Letter

  • Once a hiring decision is made, send the offer letter through UKG Ready as normal.
  • Follow the standard offer letter process — no changes for rehires at this step.
4
Background Check

Initiate the Background Check

  • Once the offer is accepted, initiate the background check through HireRight as normal.
  • Wait for the background check to return a clear result before proceeding to HR Actions.
💡
Do not proceed to the HR Actions step until the background check is complete. The rehire step in UKG Ready happens after the background check clears.
HR Actions

Processing the Rehire in UKG Ready

Once the background check is complete, follow these steps to process the rehire through HR Actions.

1
Navigation

Navigate to HR Actions

From the UKG Ready home page, follow this path:

Employees› Manage (hover)› HR Actions
2
Action

Start the Rehire Action

  • Locate Rehire in the list of available HR Actions.
  • Click the Start button next to Rehire.
3
Search

Search for the Employee

  • In the search field, type the rehire's name to pull up their existing employee record.
  • Select the correct employee from the search results.
⚠️
Make sure you are selecting the correct existing record. This links the rehire back to their prior employment history in the system.
4
Account Status

Change Account Status to Active

  • Locate the Account Status field.
  • Change the value to Active.
5
Rehire Date

Enter the Rehire Date

  • Enter the employee's rehire date in the designated date field.
  • Once the account status and rehire date are updated, you are ready to continue with the remaining hire steps.
Completing the Hire

Continue as Normal

After updating the account status and rehire date, all remaining steps follow the standard Hire an Employee Guide.

What to Complete — Per the Hire an Employee Guide

★Add the employee's position
★Enter compensation details
★Complete all remaining required fields as outlined in the Hire an Employee Guide
💡
Everything from this point forward is identical to hiring a new employee. Refer to the Hire an Employee Guide for all remaining steps including position, compensation, and any additional required fields.

Questions about the rehire process?

If you're unsure whether a former employee is eligible for rehire, contact the HR team before proceeding.
