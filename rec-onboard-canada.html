an Employee

Monitoring onboarding checklist completion for Canadian stores in UKG Ready

When to Use Access Checklists Check Completion
Overview

When to Use This Guide

Use this guide once a new employee has been hired in UKG Ready and their onboarding checklist has been assigned.

✅

Employee has been hired in UKG Ready

📋

Onboarding checklist has been assigned to the new hire

👀

You want to check whether the employee has completed their onboarding tasks

🍁

Canadian stores: I-9 and E-Verify are U.S. federal requirements and do not apply to Canada. Your only onboarding action in UKG Ready is to monitor checklist completion.

Step 1

Access the Checklist Report

Navigate to the Checklist report in UKG Ready to view onboarding status for all your new hires.

  1. 1
    Click ☰ Menu in the top left This opens the main navigation menu.
  2. 2
    Go to Team → HR → Checklists This opens the Checklist report where all employee checklists are listed.
  3. 3
    Go to the Category column and type "Lids New Hire Onboarding Checklist" Filtering by this category shows all new hire onboarding checklists for every employee you have hired.
Step 2

Check Checklist Completion

Once you've filtered to the Lids New Hire Onboarding Checklist, you can see the completion status for each employee.

100%

All tasks have been completed by both the employee and manager. No further action needed.

<100%

Tasks are still outstanding. Follow up with the employee to make sure they complete their onboarding steps.

💡

The checklist will not reach 100% until both the employee completes their tasks (forms and acknowledgements) and the manager completes their tasks. Follow up with employees who remain below 100% after their first week.
