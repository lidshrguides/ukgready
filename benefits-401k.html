401(k) Withdrawals

Online Distributions

🇺🇸 US Employees Only

Request withdrawals or rollovers online through your Vanguard account.

How to request a withdrawal or rollover
1
Log in at my.vanguardplan.com → click Plans → select Distributions → then Withdrawals
2
Review your options: Explore withdrawals (cash distribution) or Explore rollovers (move to another retirement account like an IRA)
3
Follow the on-screen instructions to complete your request
⚠️
Still employed and under 59½? Online withdrawals may not be available. Download the Hardship Distribution Form from the Forms page at my.vanguardplan.com. Early withdrawals are generally subject to a 10% penalty plus income taxes.

Manage Distributions at Vanguard

Log in and go to Plans → Distributions → Withdrawals  ·  Questions? Call Vanguard Participant Services
