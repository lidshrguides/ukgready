🇺🇸
The Lids 401(k) Salary Deferral Plan is available to United States employees only. You must be at least 18 years old and have worked at least 3 months to be eligible. Union and leased employees are excluded.

Investments

How to Invest Your Money

You have three approaches to choose from. You can change your elections at any time at my.vanguardplan.com.

🎯

By Default — if you don't choose, your savings go into a Target Date Fund based on your retirement year. It's a solid starting point.

🗺️

By Design — choose a Target Date Fund or a Brighton Jones risk model (Aggressive, Balanced, Moderate, or Conservative).

🔧

By Myself — build your own mix from the full lineup of Vanguard, DFA, Fidelity, and PIMCO funds.

💡
Not sure where to start? A Target Date Fund matching your expected retirement year is the simplest, most hands-off option. It automatically gets more conservative as you approach retirement. Find yours at my.vanguardplan.com.

How to request a loan online
1
Log in at my.vanguardplan.com → click Shortcuts in the top navigation → select Model a New Loan
2
In the Loan Center, click Loan Modeler → complete all fields → click Calculate to see your estimated payment per paycheck
3
Click Generate & Review Paperwork to review your loan agreement, then click Submit Loan / I Accept Loan Terms

Withdrawals

Online Distributions

You can request withdrawals or rollovers online through your Vanguard account. Eligibility depends on your age and employment status.

How to request a withdrawal or rollover
1
Log in at my.vanguardplan.com → click Plans in the top navigation → select Distributions → then Withdrawals
2
Review your available options — select Explore withdrawals (cash distribution) or Explore rollovers (move to another retirement account like an IRA)
3
Follow the on-screen instructions to complete your request
