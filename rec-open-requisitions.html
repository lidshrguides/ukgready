Step 1

Click Add New — Then Do the Template First

The very first thing you do after clicking Add New is select the Job Requisition Template. Do not touch anything else first.

🚨 Do This First — Before Anything Else

The Job Requisition Template auto-fills almost every field on this form. If you fill in fields before selecting the template, the template will overwrite them and you'll have to redo your work — or worse, submit incorrect information.

Click Add New on the Job Requisition list page

⚠️
Some states legally require a pay range on job postings. If your store is in one of those states, enter the min and max. If not, leave this section blank.
🚫 Do Not Touch These Sections

Leave Hiring Stages, Requirements, Summary, and Custom Fields completely alone. Do not add, edit, or delete anything in these sections. They are pre-configured and changing them will cause issues.

Step 3

Save & Submit

Once you've filled in the required fields, save and submit for approval.
