Step-by-Step

Open a Retail Requisition

Follow these steps in order after navigating to Job Requisitions.

  1. 1
    Click Add New This creates a blank requisition form.
  2. 2
    Click the search icon in the Job Requisition Template field This opens the template lookup window.
  3. 3
    Search by store number only Type just the store number (e.g. 0001) — do not type the role name.
  4. 4
    Select the correct position for that store Choose the right role (Sales Help, 3rd Key, ASM FT/PT, Store Manager, etc.).
  5. 5
    Click OK to override fields Confirm the pop-up — this will auto-fill most required fields from the template.
  6. 6
    Check Status and Visibility Ensure the Status is set correctly and the Visibility is configured so the req posts to the career page.
  7. 7
    Select the Background Check Package Scroll to the bottom and select the appropriate package: Lids Retail Management PG for US stores, or Canada Retail Management Package for Canadian stores.
Configuration Rules

Requisition Rules

Most fields auto-fill from the template. Only a handful need your attention.

✓ Do

  • Enter the correct number of openings
  • Check "Auto Closed When Filled"
  • Enter Hiring Manager 1
  • Add Pay Range (if required by state)

✗ Do Not

  • Select Evergreen
  • Select Remote Job
  • Enter anything in the Requirements or Summary section

◎ Optional

  • Use Visibility Dates for advance planning
Approval Process

What Happens After You Submit

Once you click submit, the process is automatic — here's what to expect.

💾

Click Save and Submit for Approval

Your req is saved and enters the approval queue.
↓
👥

Routes automatically to HR team

No action needed from you — HR receives it automatically.
↓
🟢

Requisition opens and posts to job boards

Once approved, the role is live and accepting applicants.
⚠️ You will not see applicants until the requisition has been approved by HR.
Watch Out

Common Mistakes to Avoid

These are the most frequent errors — double-check before submitting.

  • ✗

    Selecting the wrong requisition template for the store

  • ✗

    Entering an incorrect number of openings

  • ✗

    Forgetting to check "Auto Close When Filled"

  • ✗

    Marking the role as Remote or Evergreen

  • ✗

    Entering custom text in the Requirements or Summary section

  • ✗

    Not checking Status and Visibility before submitting — the req won't post if these are incorrect

  • ✗

    Forgetting to select a Background Check Package — choose Lids Retail Management PG (US) or Canada Retail Management Package (Canada)
