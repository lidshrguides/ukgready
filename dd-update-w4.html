How to add and submit federal or state tax withholding forms in UKG Ready

Overview How to Navigate Adding a Form Signing & Submitting
Overview

What You'll Do Here

Add new federal or state tax withholding forms directly in UKG Ready. All forms are completed and signed electronically — no paper required.

Federal

Employee's Withholding Certificate

W-4

Tells your employer how much federal income tax to withhold from each paycheck based on your filing status and adjustments.

State (Indiana)

Employee's Withholding Exemption and County Status Certificate

WH-4

Indiana state withholding form. Includes county tax information based on where you live and work.

ℹ️
You can add forms at any time. If you've had a life event — marriage, new dependent, second job — updating your W-4 ensures the right amount is withheld.
Navigation

Getting to Withholding

Two ways to get there.

Dashboard Fastest

1
Click the My Pay tile on the dashboard
2
Select Withholding from the dropdown
3
Click Add New to get started

Hamburger Menu

1
Click the ☰ menu at the top left
2
Click My Info → My Pay → Forms → Withholding
3
Click Add New
Process

Adding a Form

Once you're on the Withholding screen, follow these steps.

1

Click "Add New"

  • The Add New Withholding dialog will open
  • The current year is pre-selected
2

Choose Your Form

  • Forms are grouped by Federal and State
  • Click + Add next to the form you need (W-4 for federal, WH-4 for Indiana state)
  • You can add both in one session if needed
ℹ️
If you work or live in a state other than Indiana, your applicable state form will appear in the list instead.
3

Complete the Form

  • Fill out the form fields — filing status, allowances, and any additional withholding amounts
  • Review your entries before moving to the next step
  • Click Review when ready
Final Step

Signing & Submitting

All tax forms require an electronic signature before they take effect.

1

Review the Form

  • After completing the form, click Review in the top right
  • You'll see a full preview of the completed form
2

Click "Sign & Submit"

  • Click Sign & Submit in the top right
  • A confirmation dialog will appear with a legal declaration statement
3

Type Your Name to Confirm

  • Type your full name in the field to serve as your electronic signature
  • Click I Agree to submit
  • The form status will update to Active — your withholding elections are now in effect
✅
Once submitted, the form appears on your Withholding screen with an Active status and the date it was signed. You can download a PDF copy at any time.

Not sure what to enter on your W-4?

The IRS Tax Withholding Estimator at irs.gov can help you figure out the right settings based on your situation.
