2 Ways to Access Requests

Both methods take you to the same place — use whichever is fastest for you.

Mailbox Widget Option 1

Dashboard → Mailbox → My To Do Items

Go to the Dashboard
Find the My mailbox widget
Click My To Do Items
Select the pending Time Off Request arrow
The request will open for review

To Do Bell

Top navigation → Bell icon → To Do Items

Click the 🔔 To Do Bell at the top right
Find the Time Off Request in your list
Click the item to open the request details
Review

Reviewing the Request

When viewing a time off request, you will see the following information. Review carefully before taking action.

FieldWhat It Shows
Employee NameThe employee who submitted the request
Requested Dates/HoursThe specific date(s) and total hours requested
Time Off TypeThe type of leave requested (PTO, Sick, etc.)
Employee CommentsAny explanation the employee included with the request
⚠️
Review carefully before taking action. Approved requests will deduct from the employee's available PTO balance.
Take Action

Approve or Reject

Once you've reviewed the request, take action using the buttons at the bottom of the request.

✓ To Approve

  • Click Approve to approve the request
  • The time off is applied to the employee's schedule
  • The employee is notified automatically

✕ To Reject

  • Click Reject to deny the request
  • A Comment is required when rejecting
  • The employee is notified automatically with your comment
🚫
A comment is required when rejecting a request. The rejection will not go through without one.
