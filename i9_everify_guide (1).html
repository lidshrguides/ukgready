I-9 & E-Verify: Manager Guide

A step-by-step reference for completing the I-9 and E-Verify process for new hires in UKG Ready

Before You Begin Step 1 — Approve Docs Step 2 — Open Checklist Step 3 — Form I-9 Part 2 Step 4 — E-Verify E-Verify Outcomes
Overview

Before You Begin

As a manager, you will receive three things after your new hire is set up in the system. Here is what to expect and the order you must follow.

⚠️
Do not start any of these steps until the employee has fully completed their side first — they must finish Section 1 of the I-9 and upload photos of their identity documents before you can proceed.
Once the employee completes their portion, you will receive:
🔔 Bell / To-Do
"Upload I-9 Work Authorization Documents" — this is your prompt to review and approve the employee's document photos.
✅ Checklist
"New Hire Onboarding" checklist — contains two tasks: Process Form I-9 and Process E-Verify. You must complete them in that order.
Step 1

Approve the Document Upload

Find and open the To-Do notification, review the employee's uploaded document photos, then approve.

1
Open the To-Do item
Click the bell icon (🔔) in the top right corner of UKG Ready. Find the notification titled "Upload I-9 Work Authorization Documents" and click to open it.
The Approve/Reject HR Action Request screen — what you'll see after opening the To-Do item
1 · Click "View Details" 2 · Click the 3 dots (…) under Actions 3 · Click "Preview"
2
Preview & verify the document photos
Review each uploaded photo. Make sure the images are clear, legible, and show the full document.
The document upload screen — verify the employee has uploaded their documents here

The employee must have provided one of the following:

List A — 1 document
(Passport, Perm. Resident Card, etc.)
OR
List B + List C — 2 documents
(e.g. Driver's License + Social Security Card)
🔗
Full list of acceptable documents: uscis.gov/i-9-central/form-i-9-acceptable-documents
3
Click Approve
Once you have confirmed the photos are clear and the correct documents were provided, go back to the To-Do item and click Approve.
Step 2

Open the New Hire Onboarding Checklist

After approving the documents, navigate to your checklists to complete the I-9 and E-Verify tasks.

1
Go to My Checklists
Click My Checklists in the top navigation. Find the New Hire Onboarding – Corporate/DC checklist for the specific employee.
The New Hire Onboarding checklist — locate the one for your specific employee
Click "Open Checklist" at the bottom left
ℹ️
The checklist contains two tasks: Process Form I-9 and Process E-Verify. Complete them in that order — I-9 first, then E-Verify.
Step 3

Process Form I-9 — Complete Part 2

Open the I-9 task from the checklist, find the employee's record, complete Part 2 using the documents they provided, then sign and verify.

1
Click "Process Form I-9" in the checklist
This opens the I-9 list — a grid showing all employees who have completed their side of the I-9 under you.
The I-9 list — search and find your employee here
1 · Search the employee's name in the filter 2 · Check their checkbox 3 · Click "View" to open Part 2
🚫
Do NOT click "Mark as Complete" yet. You must fully complete Part 2 and then click Sign & Verify before marking it complete. Marking it complete before signing will lock the form incorrectly.
2
Complete Part 2 — enter the documents
Enter the document information from what the employee physically showed you. Use List A or List B + C.
List A document fields
Certification section — enter First Day of Employment and your name
ℹ️
In the Certification section: enter the employee's First Day of Employment, then enter your full name and title in the Last Name / First Name / Title field as the authorized representative.
3
Sign & Verify — then Mark as Complete
Scroll back to the top of the form. Click "Sign & Verify". Only after signing, click "Mark as Complete".
The action buttons at the top of the I-9 form — Sign & Verify must be clicked before Mark as Complete
✅
The correct order is: Complete Part 2 → Scroll to top → Sign & Verify → Mark as Complete. Do not skip or reverse these steps.
Step 4

Process E-Verify

Return to the checklist and complete the E-Verify task for the employee.

1
Click "Process E-Verify" in the checklist
Go back to the New Hire Onboarding checklist. Click Process E-Verify. On the E-Verify page, click Add New in the top right.
The E-Verify page — click "Add New" in the top right corner
Click "Add New" (top right)
2
Enter the employee's information and submit
Type the employee's name and click Next. Most fields will auto-fill from the I-9 you just completed.
ℹ️
You only need to select two things: the employee's citizenship status and the document they provided. Everything else auto-fills from the I-9. Then click Submit at the top of the page.
3
Mark as Complete
Once submitted, return to the checklist and click Mark as Complete on the E-Verify task.
E-Verify

Result Outcomes

Results typically return immediately after submission. Here is what each status means and what to do.

✅
Employment Authorized
The employee is authorized to work. No further action needed. You're done.
⏳
Pending / Case in Continuance
The case is still being processed. Check back on the E-Verify page regularly to monitor the updated status. Do not take any action until a final result is returned.
⚠️
Tentative Non-Confirmation (TNC)
Follow the on-screen steps carefully. Double-check that all information entered matches the I-9 exactly — a typo or mismatch is the most common cause. Do not take adverse action against the employee based on a TNC alone.
🚫
Final Non-Confirmation (FNC)
Contact HR immediately at hr@lids.com — do not proceed without guidance.
