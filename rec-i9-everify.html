I-9 & E-Verify: Manager Guide

Complete after the employee finishes their portion in UKG Ready

Overview

Before You Begin

⚠️
Wait for the employee first. They must complete Section 1 and upload document photos before you start.
1 · Bell
"Upload I-9 Work Authorization Documents" — review & approve the employee's document photos.
2 · Checklist
New Hire Onboarding — two tasks: Process Form I-9, then Process E-Verify.
Step 1

Approve the Document Upload

1

Open the To-Do item

Click the bell 🔔 → open "Upload I-9 Work Authorization Documents"

2

Verify the photos

Confirm photos are clear. Employee must have provided:

List A — 1 doc
Passport, Perm. Resident Card
OR
List B + C — 2 docs
Driver's License + SS Card
🔗
Full list of acceptable documents — uscis.gov
3

Click Approve

Go back to the To-Do item → click Approve.

Step 2

Open the Onboarding Checklist

1

My Checklists → New Hire Onboarding

Find the checklist for the employee → click Open Checklist.

Step 3

Process Form I-9 — Part 2

1

Find the employee → click View

🚫
Do NOT click "Mark as Complete" yet. Sign & Verify first.
2

Complete Part 2

Enter the documents shown. Fill in the Certification section.

3

Sign & Verify → Mark as Complete

Scroll to top → Sign & Verify → then Mark as Complete.

✅
Part 2 → Scroll up → Sign & Verify → Mark as Complete
Step 4

Process E-Verify

1

Click "Process E-Verify" → Add New

Back in the checklist → Process E-Verify → click Add New (top right).

2

Enter name → Next → Submit

Type employee name → Next. Most fields auto-fill from the I-9.

  • Select citizenship status
  • Select document provided
  • Click Submit at the top
3

Mark as Complete

Return to checklist → Mark as Complete.

E-Verify

Result Outcomes

✅
Employment Authorized
Done. No action needed.
⏳
Pending / Case in Continuance
Check back regularly until a final result is returned.
⚠️
Tentative Non-Confirmation (TNC)
Follow on-screen steps. Verify all info matches the I-9. Do not take adverse action.
🚫
Final Non-Confirmation (FNC)
Contact hr@lids.com before any action.
