Overview

When to Use This Guide

Use this guide once you have selected a candidate and are ready to extend a formal offer.

✅

A candidate has been selected for hire

📄

You are ready to create and send an offer letter

Step 1

Move to Offer Stage

Before creating an offer, make sure the candidate is in the correct hiring stage.

  1. 1
    Open the candidate's application Navigate to the requisition, click the Applications tab, select the candidate, and click View.
  2. 2
    Click the Offer hiring stage circle This moves the candidate to the Offer stage. Leave the date as-is and click OK to confirm.
Step 2

Add Offer & Enter Details

Once the candidate is in the Offer stage, create the offer from the Quick Actions panel.

  1. 1
    Click Add Offer Found in the Quick Actions panel on the right side of the applicant record.
  2. 2
    Review the pre-filled fields Applicant, Job Application, and Job Requisition will auto-fill — verify they are correct.
  3. 3
    Enter the Rate Type the candidate's hourly pay rate in the Rate field.
  4. 4
    Change "Per" to Hour The Per field may default to something other than Hour — make sure it is set to Hour before continuing.
  5. 5
    Enter the Start Date Enter the candidate's intended first day of work.
Step 3

Select the Offer Letter Template

Use the correct template for the role and location — using the wrong one will result in an incorrect offer letter being sent to the candidate.

🍁

Canadian stores: Do not use the Retail templates for Canadian employees. Always select the Canada | Hourly template regardless of role. Retail templates are for U.S. stores only.

  1. 1
    Click the Offer Letter Template field This opens the template selection window.
  2. 2
    Select the correct template for the role and location Use the table above to find the right one. Templates starting with "Retail" are U.S. only.
  3. 3
    Click OK to override Confirm the pop-up to apply the template to the offer.
Step 4

Finish & Submit the Offer

Before submitting, always preview the offer letter to confirm all details are correct.

  1. 1
    Preview the offer letter Click Preview to review the full offer letter before submitting — verify the name, rate, start date, and job title are all correct.
  2. 2
    Click Save and Submit This submits the offer into the approval workflow. Do not click Submit without previewing first.
⚠️
Always preview before submitting. Once submitted, the offer enters the approval workflow and cannot be easily recalled if there is an error in the details.
What Happens Next

Offer Approval Workflow

Once you submit, the process is handled automatically. Here is exactly what to expect — and what you do not need to do.

📤

You submit the offer

The offer enters the approval queue automatically.
↓
👤

Routes to your direct manager (Manager 1) for approval

Your manager receives a notification to review and approve or reject the offer. You do not need to follow up — the system notifies them automatically.
↓
📬

You receive an email notification with the outcome

You will be notified whether the offer was approved or rejected by your manager. If approved, you do not need to take any further action at this point.
↓
👥

HR receives a notification and assigns the offer to the candidate

Once your manager approves, HR is automatically notified and takes over — they will assign the offer to the candidate to ensure compliance and confirm every candidate receives a formal offer before hire. You do not need to assign the offer yourself.
↓
📩

You receive a final notification once the candidate responds

You will be notified when the candidate signs or declines the offer. At that point, refer to the After Candidate Responds section below for your next steps.
💡

Your role in the workflow is done after you submit. Wait for the notification that tells you the candidate has responded — HR handles everything in between.

After the Offer

After the Candidate Responds

Once you receive the notification that the candidate has signed or declined, here is what to do next.

✓ Offer Accepted

  • ✓ Move candidate to Initiate Background Check if a background check is required for the position
  • ✓ Move candidate to Preboarding directly if no background check applies

✗ Offer Declined

  • ✗ Leave the candidate in the Offer stage — do not move them
  • ✗ Do NOT move them to the Hired stage
Watch Out

Common Errors to Avoid

These are the most frequent mistakes when creating offers — double-check each one before submitting.

  • ✗

    Selecting the wrong offer letter template for the role or location

  • ✗

    Using a Retail template for a Canadian employee — always use Canada | Hourly

  • ✗

    Leaving "Per" set to something other than Hour

  • ✗

    Skipping the offer preview before submitting

  • ✗

    Entering an incorrect pay rate or start date

  • ✗

    Moving a candidate to the next stage before the offer is accepted

  • ✗

    Moving a declined candidate to the Hired stage
