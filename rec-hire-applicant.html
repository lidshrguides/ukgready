Step 4

Contact Information

Contact details pre-fill from the application. Verify email, cell phone, and country code — then click Continue.

💡
U.S. and Canada are both +1 — make sure you select the correct country from the dropdown, not just the code.

Step 8

Managers & Cost Centers — Submit

Select the employee's direct manager, verify the cost center autofilled correctly, then click Submit.

💡
Once submitted, the hire action routes through the approval workflow. The employee record is created once approved.
Rehires

Processing a Rehire

Do not use this guide for rehires. Confirm the offer was signed and background check completed, then use the Rehire action in HR Actions.

✗
