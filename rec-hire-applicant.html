HR Actions:
Hire an Employee

A step-by-step reference for completing the hire action for a new retail employee in UKG Ready

When to Use Open Preboarding Form Hire the Candidate Effective Date Rule Employee Information Contact Information Work Information Compensation Managers & Submit Rehires Only
Overview

When to Use This Guide

This guide covers the HR Actions hire workflow — used only for brand new employees who do not yet exist in UKG Ready.

✅

Hiring a brand new employee

👤

Employee does not yet exist in UKG Ready

✗

Do NOT use for rehires — use the Rehire action instead

✗

Do NOT use for job changes — use the Job Change action instead

Before You Begin

Open the Preboarding Form

Before starting the hire action, you need to retrieve the candidate's SSN/SIN and Date of Birth from their completed preboarding form. Open this in a separate window so you can reference it while completing the hire form.

  1. 1
    Click your initials in the top right corner → New Window This opens UKG Ready in a second browser window so you can reference preboarding while completing the hire form.
  2. 2
    Navigate to ☰ Menu → Team → Recruitment → Applicant Checklists This takes you to the list of applicant checklists.
  3. 3
    Select the candidate's checkbox → Click View Find the candidate in the list, check their box, and click View in the top right.
  4. 4
    Select Preboarding Open the Preboarding checklist to access the candidate's SSN/SIN and Date of Birth. Keep this window open — you will need it in the next steps.
⚠️
Do NOT collect SSN, SIN, or Date of Birth outside of UKG Ready. Only access this information through the preboarding form for the purpose of completing the hire action.
Step 1

Hire the Candidate

Return to the candidate's application and launch the hire action from Quick Actions.

  1. 1
    Open the candidate's application Navigate to the requisition, click the Applications tab, select the candidate, and click View.
  2. 2
    Click Hire in the Quick Actions panel Found on the right side of the applicant record.
  3. 3
    Select Retail Leader - Hire Employee This launches the hire action form. The candidate will move to the Hired stage automatically once this is completed.
Step 2

Effective Date Rule

The Effective From date applies to both Position Assignment and Compensation. This rule must be followed every time.

FieldWhat to EnterNotes
Effective From Today's date Applies to both Position Assignment and Compensation sections
⚠️
Do NOT backdate or postdate. Always use today's date as the Effective From date regardless of when the employee's actual start date is. Start date is entered separately.
Step 3

Employee Information

Enter the employee's personal details carefully. Reference the preboarding form you opened earlier for SSN/SIN and Date of Birth.

FieldWhat to EnterNotes
Date of Birth From preboarding form Copy exactly from the candidate's completed preboarding form
SSN or SIN From preboarding form U.S. employees provide SSN — Canadian employees provide SIN
First Name Legal first name Must match legal documents — not a preferred name
Last Name Legal last name Must match legal documents
Nickname / Preferred Name If applicable Optional — enter only if the employee has provided one
Employee ID Auto Leave blank Auto-populates — do not enter anything
Username Auto Leave blank Auto-populates — do not enter anything
Password Auto Leave blank Auto-populates — do not enter anything
Hire Date Required Employee's start date This is the employee's actual first day of work — not today's date
Primary Language Required Select from dropdown Important: This determines what language the employee receives their onboarding forms in. Always confirm the employee's preferred language and select accordingly.
🚫
Do NOT enter Employee ID, Username, or Password. These fields auto-populate — entering anything in them will cause errors in the system.
💡

After entering all employee information, click Continue to move to the next section.

Step 4

Contact Information

The candidate's contact details should pre-fill from their application. Verify and then continue.

FieldWhat to DoNotes
Primary Email Verify it is pre-filled Should auto-fill from the candidate's application — do not change unless incorrect
Cell Phone Verify it is pre-filled Should auto-fill from the candidate's application
Country Code Required Select correct country code U.S. = +1 / Canada = +1 (but select Canada specifically from the dropdown)
💡

After verifying contact information, click Continue to move to Work Information.

Step 5

Work Information — Position Assignment

Assign the employee to their position. Search by store number to find the correct position.

  1. 1
    Click +Add Position This opens the position assignment form in the Position Assignments section.
  2. 2
    Click the search icon under the Position field This opens the position lookup window.
  3. 3
    Search by store number only Type just the store number to find all positions for that location.
  4. 4
    Select the correct position for the employee's role Choose the position that matches the role they were hired into.
FieldWhat to EnterNotes
Start Date Required Today's date Use today's date — this is the effective date of the position assignment, not the employee's start date
Is Primary Assignment Required Check the box Must be checked for all new hires
Assignment Change Reason Required New Hire Select New Hire from the dropdown
Assigned FTE Required 1 Enter 1 for all retail new hires regardless of part-time or full-time status
⚠️
Primary Manager: After saving the position, a prompt may appear to select the position's primary manager. Select Directly Reports to Manager. If no one appears in the list, click Cancel and Continue — do not leave this hanging.
🚫
Do NOT enter compensation in the position details section. Compensation is entered separately in the Base Compensation section below.
Step 6

Work Information — Base Compensation

Enter the employee's pay rate in the Base Compensation section — not in the position details. Follow the field rules exactly.

  1. 1
    Click +Add in the Base Compensation section This opens the Add New Base Compensation pop-up.
FieldWhat to EnterNotes
Effective Date Required Today's date Same rule as always — do not backdate or postdate
Reason Code Required New Hire Select New Hire from the dropdown
Amount Required Employee's hourly rate Enter the pay rate from the offer letter
Per (first field) Required Hour Change this to Hour — it may default to something else. This is the only Per field you touch.
Hours Leave Alone Do not change Leave whatever value is already there — do not edit
Per (second field) Leave Alone Do not change Leave whatever value is already there — do not edit
PP in a Year Leave Alone Do not change Leave whatever value is already there — do not edit
Amount Earning Autopay Leave Alone Do not change Leave whatever value is already there — do not edit
Autopay Type Leave Alone Do not change Leave whatever value is already there — do not edit
Step 7

Managers & Cost Centers — Submit

The final step before submitting through the approval workflow.

FieldWhat to EnterNotes
Manager 1 Required Employee's direct manager Search and select the employee's direct manager — this is who they report to day-to-day
Default Lids Corporation Auto Should auto-fill The cost center should populate automatically — verify it is correct before submitting
  1. 1
    Verify Manager 1 and cost center are correct Double-check before submitting — these drive payroll and reporting.
  2. 2
    Click Submit This sends the hire action through the approval workflow. The employee record will be created once approved.
Rehires Only

Processing a Rehire After Background Check

If the employee is a rehire, do not continue through the standard hire steps after initiating the background check. Follow these steps instead.

✗

Do NOT complete the full hire action for a rehire — use the Rehire HR Action instead after the background check is initiated.

  1. 1
    Initiate the background check Complete only the background check initiation as normal. Stop there — do not proceed with the hire action.
  2. 2
    Go to HR Actions via the search bar Type HR Actions in the UKG Ready search bar and select it from the results.
  3. 3
    Click Start next to Rehire Locate the Rehire action and click Start.
  4. 4
    Complete the required fields Enter the following — these are the only fields that need to be updated:
FieldWhat to Enter
Rehire Date RequiredEnter the employee's rehire date
Status RequiredChange to Active
New Position RequiredEnter the employee's new position
New Compensation RequiredEnter the employee's new pay rate
