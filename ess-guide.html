Self-Service Action

Update All Personal Information

Use this action to review and update your name, address, email, preferred language, date of birth, and EEO information.

1

Start the Action

  • On the HR Actions page, click Start next to Update All Personal Information
2

Review & Update Each Section

  • Review and update your information as needed — including name, address, email, preferred language, and date of birth
  • This action may also include EEO information, which supports reporting and compliance
3

Navigate and Submit

  • Click Continue to move through each section
  • Click Submit when finished
Self-Service Action

Update My Address

Use this action to update your home address. Address changes may affect your state and local tax withholding.

1

Start the Action

  • On the HR Actions page, click Start next to Update My Address
2

Enter Your Updated Address

  • Enter your updated home address and submit the change
3

Update Tax Withholding If Needed

  • If your address change impacts state or local taxes, you may need to complete new tax withholding forms
  • Navigate to: My HR → Forms → Government Forms → Withholding → click +Add New
⚠️
Why this matters: Tax withholding is based on where you live and work. Updating your address ensures the correct state and local taxes are applied to your pay.
Self-Service Action

Update My Direct Deposit

Use this action to add or update your bank account information for direct deposit.

1

Start the Action

  • On the HR Actions page, click Start next to Update My Direct Deposit
2

Add Your Bank Account

  • Click +Add to enter new bank account information
  • Enter your routing and account numbers
3

Submit

  • Click Submit when complete
🏦
If you have more than one account, list them in this order:
Account 1: Flat $ Amount  |  Account 2: Entire/Remainder

Self-Service Action

Update My Emergency Contacts

Use this action to add or update the emergency contacts on your employee record.

1

Start the Action

  • On the HR Actions page, click Start next to Update My Emergency Contacts
2

Add a Contact

  • Click +Add on the right side of the screen
  • Check the box to select the contact type: Emergency
  • Enter the contact's name, relationship type, phone number, and email address
3

Submit

  • Click Submit when at least 1 contact has been entered

Self-Service Action

Upload I-9 Work Authorization Documents

Use this action to upload documents verifying your identity and work authorization for Form I-9.

1

Start the Action

  • On the HR Actions page, click Start next to Upload I-9 Work Authorization Documents
2

Review Acceptable Documents

  • Before uploading, review the official USCIS List of Acceptable Documents
  • You must upload either:
      → One document from List A
         OR
      → One document from List B AND one document from List C
3

Upload Your Documents

  • Ensure all uploaded documents are clear, legible, and unexpired
  • Submit once all required documents are uploaded
🚫
Do not upload expired documents. All documents must be current and valid.
