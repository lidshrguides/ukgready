Compliance

I-9 Compliance Rules

Before you touch the I-9 in UKG Ready, understand these non-negotiable rules. Violations carry federal penalties.

!
Must be completed on the employee's first dayThe I-9 cannot be completed before the employee's start date, and must not be delayed past day one.
!
Documents must be reviewed in personYou must physically examine the original documents — not copies, not photos, not digital uploads alone.
!
Employee cannot start work without a completed I-9This is a federal legal requirement. An employee may not begin working until I-9 Section 2 is complete.
🚫
DO NOT complete Section 2 of the I-9 without first reviewing documents in person. Completing it remotely or based on uploaded images alone is a compliance violation.
Step 1

Review Uploaded Documents

When the employee uploads their I-9 documents, you will receive a To Do notification. This is a digital preview only — you must still verify originals in person.

  1. 1
    Receive a To Do notification to review documentsAppears in your To Do list (bell icon) or Mailbox when the employee completes their upload.
  2. 2
    Open the approval requestClick the To Do item to open the Approve/Reject HR Action Request for the I-9 document upload.
  3. 3
    Click View Details → PreviewReview what the employee uploaded. Confirm documents appear clear and legible.
  4. 4
    Confirm documents are clear, then click ApproveApproving acknowledges the upload — it does not replace your in-person verification obligation.
⚠️
Approving the upload is not the same as completing the I-9. Documents must still be reviewed in person when you complete I-9 Section 2.
Step 2

Open the Employee's Checklist

Access the employee's onboarding checklist to find and complete the I-9 task.

  1. 1
    Navigate to My ChecklistsTwo paths: Home Page → Mailbox → My Checklists, or click the bell (To Do) icon → My Checklists.
  2. 2
    Locate the employee's onboarding checklistFind the "Lids New Hire Onboarding Checklist" assigned to this employee.
  3. 3
    Click Open ChecklistThis opens the full checklist where you can access the I-9 task and other onboarding items.
Step 3

Complete I-9 Section 2

Section 2 must be completed with the employee present and original documents in hand. Must be done within 3 business days of the employee's start date.

  1. 1
    Open the I-9 task from the checklistLocate and click the I-9 task within the employee's onboarding checklist.
  2. 2
    Review Section 1Verify the employee completed Section 1 with their personal information. Do not edit Section 1.
  3. 3
    Verify original documents in personPhysically examine the employee's original identity and work authorization documents. Do not accept copies.
  4. 4
    Complete Section 2 in UKG ReadyEnter the document details and certify Section 2 based on what you reviewed in person.

Acceptable Documents

List A

1 document from List A — establishes both identity and work authorization.

Example: U.S. Passport, Permanent Resident Card

OR

List B + List C

1 from List B (identity) AND 1 from List C (work authorization).

Example: Driver's License + Social Security Card

⚠️
Documents must match — both what was uploaded AND what is presented in person.

If the documents differ, do not complete Section 2. Contact HR before proceeding.

Step 4

E-Verify Training & Test

Before you can submit E-Verify cases, you must complete training and pass the test. This is a one-time requirement per user.

  1. 1
    Go to My Checklists and locate the employeeNavigate to My Checklists from your home page or To Do bell.
  2. 2
    Select Process E-VerifyThis launches the E-Verify workflow for this employee.
  3. 3
    Complete the E-Verify training tutorialClick Start Tutorial and work through the full training before submitting any cases.
  4. 4
    Pass the test with 80% or higherClick Start Test once you've completed the tutorial. You must score 80%+ to be authorized to submit cases.
Step 5

Submit E-Verify Case

Submit after — and only after — you have completed I-9 Section 2.

  1. 1
    Search for and select the employeeNavigate to HR → Employee Maintenance → E-Verify and search for the employee.
  2. 2
    Complete the verification formFill in employee name, SSN, Date of Birth, Hire Date, citizenship status, and document type. Everything must match the completed I-9 exactly.
  3. 3
    Click ContinueThis submits the case. The system will connect to E-Verify and return a result.
⚠️
Always submit E-Verify after completing I-9 Section 2 — never before.
E-Verify Results

E-Verify Results

Once submitted, E-Verify will return one of these results. Know what each one means.

  • Employment Authorized

    Identity and work authorization confirmed. No further action needed — onboarding can continue.

  • TNC

    Tentative Nonconfirmation — E-Verify could not confirm the information submitted. Follow the instructions displayed on screen in UKG Ready — the system will walk you through the required next steps.

  • FNC

    Final Nonconfirmation — E-Verify was unable to confirm work authorization after the TNC process. Follow the instructions displayed on screen in UKG Ready — the system will guide you through what to do next.

🚫
Do NOT take independent action on a TNC or FNC result. Read and follow all instructions displayed on screen in UKG Ready — the system will guide you through each required step.
Checklist Monitoring

Onboarding Checklist Status

Monitor checklist completion to make sure every new hire finishes their onboarding tasks. The checklist is not 100% complete until both the employee and manager complete their steps.

  1. 1
    Go to ☰ Menu → Team → HR → ChecklistsThis opens the Checklist report for all employees you manage.
  2. 2
    Go to the Category column and type "Lids New Hire Onboarding Checklist"This filters to show only new hire onboarding checklists across everyone you've hired.
  3. 3
    Check the completion percentage for each employee100% means all tasks are done. Anything less means the employee or manager still has outstanding items.
💡
Follow up with any employee who remains below 100% after their first week. Both the employee and manager must complete their respective checklist tasks before it will show as fully complete.
⚠️ Critical — Read This

Position and Compensation: Do Not Skip These Steps

Two of the most common onboarding errors — both of which cause major downstream problems. Every new hire must have both confirmed before onboarding is complete.

🚫
If position or compensation is missing or wrong, do NOT proceed. Fix it before moving on.

An incorrect or missing position will prevent the employee from appearing in Storeforce. An employee who doesn't exist in Storeforce cannot be scheduled.

Position Requirements

  1. 1
    Position must follow the correct format The position must be the 4 digits of the store number — followed by their job title. Example: 0123 - Sales Associate. If the format is wrong, the employee will not appear in Storeforce.
  2. 2
    Check the "Is Primary Assignment" box When assigning the position, you MUST check the box that says "Is Primary Assignment." If this box is not checked, the employee will NOT show up in Storeforce — even if everything else is correct.

Compensation Requirements

  1. 1
    Compensation must be entered before the employee's first pay period An employee without compensation entered cannot be paid correctly. Confirm pay rate, pay type (hourly or salary), and effective date are all set.
💡
If you are unsure whether the position or compensation was set correctly, contact hr@lids.com before the employee's first scheduled shift.
