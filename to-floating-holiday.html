How to submit a Floating Holiday time-off request in UKG Ready

Overview How to Submit
Overview

What Is a Floating Holiday?

Floating Holidays are a separate time-off bank provided to Corporate employees each year.

📍 Corporate employees only

The Basics

  • Corporate employees receive 16 hours (2 days) per year
  • Provided at the beginning of the year (1/1)
  • Separate bank from PTO — does not mix

When They Apply

  • Floating Holidays are provided in place of company holidays on Christmas Eve and Black Friday
  • You may work those days and use Floating Holidays on other dates, OR use them on Christmas Eve and/or Black Friday
Process

How to Submit a Floating Holiday Request

Follow each step in order. Your request will go to your manager for approval.

1

Access Time Off Request

On the Home Page, click the My Time icon, then select Request Time Off.

2

Select Floating Holiday as the Time Off Type

Click the magnifying glass next to the Time Off Type field and select Floating Holiday.

3

Click Start Request

Click Start Request to proceed.

4

Select Full Day

Choose Full Day as the request type.

5

Add a Comment (Optional)

Add any relevant notes for your manager if applicable.

6

Submit Request

Click Submit Request. Your manager will be notified to approve or deny.
