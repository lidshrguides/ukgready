Step-by-Step

How to View Applicants

Navigate to your open requisition first, then follow these steps.

  1. 1
    Click the Applications tab on the requisition This is the second tab at the top of the requisition page, next to Job Req. Info.
  2. 2
    Select the applicant's checkbox Check the box to the left of the applicant's name in the list.
  3. 3
    Click View Found in the Quick Actions panel on the right side of the screen.
💡

Quick Access Tip: Type "Job Application" in the search bar (top right) and select the 2nd option — "Job Applications / Recruitment > Candidates" — to reach the applicant list faster without navigating through the requisition.

Reviewing

What to Review

Once you open an applicant's record, focus on these three areas. Use the Jump To section on the left to navigate the page quickly.

📊

Hiring Stage

Check where the candidate currently sits in the hiring pipeline.

📄

Resume

Review the candidate's resume before making any stage decisions.

📝

Questionnaire

Review their application questionnaire responses for fit.

⚠️ Ignore the Job Application section — skip past it when reviewing an applicant record. It does not contain information you need to act on.
Hiring Pipeline

Move Candidate Stages

The stages available depend on the role you're hiring for. Find the right pipeline below, then follow the steps to move a candidate.

What Each Stage Means

Application Received Candidate applied — no action taken yet. Review their resume and questionnaire here.
Application Review Management roles only. Move here to indicate you've reviewed the application and are considering the candidate.
Interview You're scheduling or have completed an interview. Use the Communicate button to send the interview request from here.
Offer You're ready to extend an offer. See the How to Extend an Offer guide for next steps from this stage.
Initiate Background Check Offer accepted — triggers the background check process through HireRight. Do not move here until the candidate has verbally accepted.
Preboarding Background check cleared — candidate receives preboarding tasks including I-9 and new hire paperwork.
Declined Candidate is no longer being considered. Move here to remove them from your active pipeline. This may trigger a notification.
Hired Candidate has completed preboarding and is officially hired.
Position Filled All openings on the requisition have been filled. The req will auto-close if "Auto Closed When Filled" was checked when it was created.
  1. 1
    Click the Hiring Stage circle you want to move the candidate to Circles appear at the top of the applicant record in the Hiring Stages section.
  2. 2
    Do not change the date Leave the Actual Date field as-is when the pop-up appears.
  3. 3
    Click OK Confirm the Hiring Stage Modification pop-up to save the change.
⚠️
Important: Moving a candidate to certain stages may automatically trigger an email to the applicant. Double-check you have selected the correct stage before clicking OK.
Communication

Send Communication

Use this to send interview requests or other messages directly to applicants through UKG.

☑️
Select the applicant's checkbox, then click Communicate The Communicate button appears in the Quick Actions panel on the right.
📋
Select a template Choose either "Request Phone Interview" or "Request In-Person Interview" — the subject, from, and body will auto-fill.
✏️
Update the "From" field to your email address This is required — the default shows a no-reply address. Replace it with your own so the applicant can respond to you directly.
📤
Click Send Review the message before sending.
Best Practices

Best Practices

Follow these habits every time you manage applicants to avoid errors and keep your pipeline clean.

  • ✓

    Always review the resume before moving a candidate to the next stage

  • ✓

    Review the questionnaire responses before making a hiring decision

  • ✓

    Use communication templates only — do not type custom messages

  • ✓

    Double-check the hiring stage before clicking OK — some stages trigger automatic emails

  • ✓

    Always update the "From" field to your email before sending any communication
