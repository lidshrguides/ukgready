Overview

What You'll Find Here

Access your pay statements at any time and estimate future paychecks — all from UKG Ready.

💡
Pay statements are available as soon as payroll is processed. You can view, download, or print any past paystub from the Pay Statements screen.
📱
On mobile? The UKG Ready app makes it easy — tap View pay stubs directly from the home dashboard.
Navigation

3 Ways to Access Pay Stubs

Use whichever method is easiest for you.

Dashboard Shortcut Fastest

Home → My Pay → Pay Statements

Log in to UKG Ready
Click the My Pay tile on the dashboard
Select Pay Statements from the dropdown

Hamburger Menu

☰ Menu → My Info → My Pay → Pay History

Click the ☰ menu at the top left
Go to My Info
Click My Pay
Select Pay History

Mobile App

Home Dashboard → View pay stubs

Open the UKG Ready app
From the home dashboard, tap View pay stubs
Feature

Predict My Pay

Estimate your take-home pay before payday based on your hours, rate, and deductions.

⚠️
Estimates only. Predict My Pay is not an actual paystub and should not be used for income verification or comparison. Accuracy improves after your first few paychecks. ZayZoon or UKG Wallet advances will not appear here.

🏠 Access from Dashboard

Click Predict my pay in the My Pay widget on the home dashboard.

☰ Access from Menu

Hamburger menu → My Info → My Pay → Predict My Pay

1

Regular Pay

  • Your hourly rate or salary auto-populates from your employee record
  • Enter your expected scheduled hours for the pay period to generate an estimate
  • Select Hourly or Salaried from the Type of Pay dropdown
2

Federal & State Taxes

  • Your tax settings auto-populate from your current withholding elections
  • You can adjust these fields to see how changes would affect your take-home pay — great for planning purposes
3

Overtime & Additional Earnings (Optional)

  • Add overtime hours or any additional earnings if applicable
  • Note: items processed once per month may need to be removed for an accurate estimate
4

Click Calculate

  • Review your estimated gross pay, net pay, taxes, and deductions
  • Your benefit deductions auto-populate based on current elections
  • Data entered here is not saved — it's for estimation only
